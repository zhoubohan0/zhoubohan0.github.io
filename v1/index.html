Bohan Zhou | 周伯涵

I'm currently a Ph.D. student (2026 - 2030) in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), supervised by James Cheng. My current research focuses on embodied intelligence and foundation models, with interests in multimodal learning, reinforcement learning and generalizable agents. Previously, I received my master's degree from Peking University (2023 - 2026), supervised by Zongqing Lu, and my bachelor's degree from Nankai University (2019 - 2023), supervised by Jingtai Liu.
