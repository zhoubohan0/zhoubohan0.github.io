The Floor Is Not the Limit: Recovering Atmospheric Backscatter from Spaceborne Lidar via Physics-Aligned Encoding

Tianchi Xu, Yuanpeng He, Jintao Xu, Kang He, Linyu Li, Huanyao Zhang, Dongming Jin, Bohan Zhou, Wenpin Jiao, Zhi Jin
Retrieving atmospheric backscatter with a parameter-free encoding derived from the lidar attenuation relation, retaining recorded low-SNR measurements below the nominal noise floor. RoboAware: Learning to Coordinate Embodied Skills from Counterfactual Outcomes

Bohan Zhou, et al. (2026)
First author and project lead. Learning state-dependent coordination between modular skills and end-to-end policies from same-state counterfactual outcomes, with low-level executors frozen. Averaged 77.0% success across reported evaluation splits covering 100 tasks on three benchmarks, with zero-shot deployment on long-horizon real-robot tasks.
